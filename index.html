Dungeon Jam

Slide the cute monsters out through doors of their own colour before the fuse reaches the TNT!

A puzzle game for iPhone by Jgutierrez Studios.

Help

How do I play? Drag a monster to slide it. It can only leave through a door of its own colour, and it needs a clear straight way out.

Where is the Daily Dungeon? It opens once you reach floor 20. Until then the DAILY button on the map shows a padlock.

I bought something and didn't get it. Open Settings (the gear, top left of the home screen) and tap RESTORE, or tap RESTORE in the shop.

I saw an ad that isn't right. Please tell us! Email a screenshot of the ad, or a few words about it, to jagutierrezgames@gmail.com and we will block it. Ads in Dungeon Jam are meant to be family-friendly.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Settings → AD CHOICES (shown in regions that need it), or your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
