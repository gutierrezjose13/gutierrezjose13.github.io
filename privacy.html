Dungeon Jam: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Dungeon Jam is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Jgutierrez Studios ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (floors cleared, power-ups, daily gifts and streaks) is saved only on your iPhone. We do not have accounts, logins, or a server that stores your progress, and we never see your name, email, contacts or photos.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the game asks), an approximate location based on your IP address, information about your device, and how you interact with ads. Ads are limited to family-friendly content. In regions with privacy laws such as the EU and UK, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time from Settings (the gear) → PRIVACY. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game records which products were bought, on your phone, so they can be handed over and restored.

Children

Dungeon Jam is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are limited to family-friendly content.

Your choices

You can refuse tracking, change your ad privacy choices in the game's Settings, or delete the game to remove everything it saved on your phone. For any question or request about your data, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
