Dungeon Jam: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Dungeon Jam is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Jgutierrez Studios ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it and why, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (floors cleared, power-ups, daily gifts and streaks, and which purchases were made) is saved only on your iPhone. We have no accounts, no logins and no server, so we never receive your progress, and we never see your name, email, contacts or photos. The game itself does not send any information to us and has no analytics.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, which is the only other company that receives information from the game. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when your iPhone asks), an approximate location based on your IP address, information about your device, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information such as crashes. Ads are limited to family-friendly content.

When you first open the game, Google's privacy message may appear, followed by Apple's question "Allow Dungeon Jam to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites?". In regions with privacy laws such as the EU and UK, Google asks for your consent before personalising ads. You can change your ad privacy choices at any time from the game's Settings (the gear) → AD CHOICES (shown in regions that need it), and you can turn off tracking for this game or all apps in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Sharing and protection

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else. Google receives the information above directly from its own code in the game, under its privacy policy and the data protection terms that apply to AdMob, which protect that information at least as well as this policy does.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game records which products were bought, on your phone, so they can be handed over and restored.

How long information is kept, and deleting it

What the game saves on your phone stays there until you delete the game, which removes it all. We keep no copy anywhere. Google keeps advertising information for the periods described in its retention policy (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

To withdraw consent, use Settings → AD CHOICES in the game (where shown) or turn off tracking in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. To ask for your data to be deleted, use Google's privacy controls described in its privacy policy, or email us and we will help: because we never receive your information, we cannot look it up ourselves, but we will pass your request on to Google.

Children

Dungeon Jam is a family-friendly game made for a general audience; it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are limited to family-friendly content. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
